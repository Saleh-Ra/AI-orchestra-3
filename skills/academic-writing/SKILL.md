## Academic writing guidelines

1. Use Markdown with `#` title, `##` chapters, and `###` sections.
2. Write primarily in Hebrew; keep English technical terms in Latin script where natural.
3. Expand outline points into full paragraphs — target ~15 pages of content when requested.
4. One idea per paragraph; use transitions between sections.
5. Do not produce LaTeX or JSON unless the task explicitly asks for that format.
